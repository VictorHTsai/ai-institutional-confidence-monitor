# Candidate universe and production eligibility

This is a research selection, not an approved production constituent list. Security identities must pass CUSIP/class/history validation. 19 issuers / 20 traded securities. Selection uses current thematic relevance and therefore has hindsight/survivorship selection bias when shown back to 2021; it is not a historical investable AI strategy backtest.

|---|---|---|

| AVGO | Compute | Custom accelerators/networking; software and M&A confound position growth. |
| MRVL | Compute | Datacenter networking/custom compute; verify current and historical CUSIPs. |

Initial fixed-2021 core candidate: the first 14 issuers, 15 securities. ORCL is an independently displayed overlay pending classification. Satellites do not enter the 2021 index. Do not inflate the count merely to reach a requested range.

Excluded initially: INTC (turnaround/foundry/general CPU exposure makes AI-specific inference weak), broad industrial utilities/electrical/cooling companies (second-order capex and non-AI end markets require a separate validated study), generic technology and semiconductor ETFs (overlap/double counting), private AI labs (not 13F public common equity), Taiwan-listed TSM ordinary shares (different reporting scope). These are research scope decisions, not negative investment judgments.

Future changes need dated rationale, eligibility, sources and a new methodology version. Additions must not mechanically increase a fixed-basket accumulation series.
